## Key Highlights

### Leading mindset in the AI era

Mr. Joseph opened the session by emphasizing that AI and robots, no matter how advanced, are still tools that support people. Humans remain the final decision makers in the innovation cycle. The key lesson was to keep a lifelong learning mindset because technology will continue changing rapidly in the coming years.

### One Team - Chatbot Agent

One Team built a multi-channel ordering AI assistant through Zalo and WhatsApp, allowing customers to order food without downloading another app or creating a new account. The team used AWS Agent Core to maintain conversation memory and reduce hallucination risk, preventing the AI from confirming incorrect orders by itself.

### Signal Scout - Multi-agent System

Signal Scout created a system that collects scattered competitor signals from the Internet, such as financial reports and public information. A multi-agent workflow then analyzes whether a business should adopt a similar model while considering risks and potential revenue.

### Plan - AI Assistant for Solution Architects

Plan built an assistant for Solution Architects that analyzes requirements written in natural language. From the initial input, the system can suggest a Draw.io cloud architecture diagram, produce a cost table, and generate Terraform code for infrastructure deployment.

### 3KA - S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D

3KA presented a crowd management system using AWS Kinesis, cameras, YOLO, ByteTrack, and an AI Agent. The system monitors crowd density in real time at places such as airports or supermarkets and recommends staff coordination actions when congestion is detected.

### Six Pillar - Adaptive Workflow Engine

Six Pillar focused on reducing false positives in anti-money laundering workflows. The team designed a multi-agent system to investigate KYC profiles and money flow, automatically classify and score cases, and escalate only truly complex cases for human review.

## What I Learned

### Product and skill perspective

- **Business context is more important than technology alone:** Even advanced technology must start from a real business problem. A valuable product should address a real pain point and solve a practical problem.
- **Scope management matters:** In a hackathon, scope must be controlled carefully. Instead of expanding too many features, teams should focus on an MVP that runs and can be demonstrated clearly.
- **A demo is stronger than an idea on paper:** A good idea becomes convincing only when there is a working product or POC to prove its value.

### Teamwork and pressure handling

- **Lowering ego:** In a high-pressure environment, clear responsibility sharing, trust in teammates, and focusing on the common goal are more important than defending personal opinions.
- **Facing pressure:** Stories about staying up late, dealing with infrastructure issues, debating inside the team, and fixing problems near demo time helped me understand the real process of building a product.

## Application to My Work

- **Action over fear:** When there is a chance to join a hackathon or try a new technology, I should not wait until I feel perfect. Real pressure can become the fastest learning environment.
- **Human-in-the-loop design:** When applying AI to finance, architecture, or operations systems, important decisions should still include human review and approval.
- **Prioritize execution:** For projects such as IRMS, I should focus on flows that can run, be tested, and be presented clearly instead of only describing ideas.

## Event Experience

The sharing session felt realistic because the teams did not only talk about successful results. They also described struggles with code, infrastructure errors, time pressure, different opinions, and how they overcame those issues to complete their demos. I realized that the greatest value of a hackathon is not only the prize, but also teamwork experience, pressure handling, and the relationships built during the process.

Although the time was short, the teams still produced practical architectures and products. This motivated me to continue improving my cloud, AI, and teamwork skills and to be ready for similar challenges in the future.
